The first release of Matrix Time Premium. Everything in Matrix Time, plus:

- Seven time sizes, from Matrix Time's own up to four times it
- A filled or hollow time, drawn with no box, so the rain falls around or through the digits
- Time alignment: left, centre or right
- Time colours: green, white, cyan, amber, orange or red
- Rain colours: green, cyan, blue, amber, orange, red or white, or a white-to-green or green-to-teal gradient
- Trail length: 25%, 50% or 75% of the screen
- A larger, bolder always-on time, with a brightness setting
- The date under the time
- Five preset slots, three of them ready to load: Green, Red and Blue
- Every setting can be changed on the watch, from the face's Customize menu, as well as in the Connect IQ app

Also fixed: the always-on screen could go dark and stay dark until the face was restarted.
